# Monetization Strategy

## Revenue Streams

### 1. API Subscriptions (Primary)

**Pricing Model: Freemium + Tiered Subscriptions**

- **Free Tier**: 100 requests/month (conversion funnel)
  - No credit card needed
  - Great for students, developers, testing
  - Shows value proposition

- **Starter Tier**: $4.99/month (5,000 requests)
  - For small projects, indie developers
  - About 160 requests/day
  - ROI: Projects using 2,500+ requests/month

- **Pro Tier**: $14.99/month (50,000 requests)
  - For businesses, production apps
  - About 1,600 requests/day
  - ROI: Projects using 25,000+ requests/month

**Projected Monthly Revenue**
- 1,000 signups: 100 free → 40 Starter ($199) + 10 Pro ($149) = ~$350
- 10,000 signups: 1,000 free → 400 Starter ($1,996) + 100 Pro ($1,499) = ~$3,500
- 100,000 signups: 10,000 free → 4,000 Starter ($19,960) + 1,000 Pro ($14,990) = ~$35,000

### 2. Enterprise Contracts (Scale)

- Custom pricing for large volume
- Dedicated support tier ($99+/month)
- SLA guarantees
- White-label options

**Estimated**: $500-5,000/month per enterprise client

### 3. Discord Bot (Passive Income)

- Free tier: Limited daily questions
- Pro: Unlimited + priority responses
- Charge per Discord server: $1.99/month

**Estimated**: $100-1,000/month if 50-500 servers adopt

### 4. Consulting & Custom Models

- Fine-tune for specific industries (legal, medical, finance)
- Charge: $500-2,000 per custom model
- Monthly retainer for updates

**Estimated**: $1,000-5,000/month

## Marketing Strategy

### Phase 2: Growth (Months 4-6)

### Phase 3: Scale (Months 7-12)
- Target tech companies
- Sales outreach for enterprise tier
- Feature partnerships
- Conferences & speaking
- Developer relations program

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) vs Lifetime Value (LTV)

**Free users converting to Starter** (assume 10% conversion):
- Monthly revenue per user: $4.99
- Lifetime (1 year): ~$60
- CAC target: < $6 (breakeven in 2-3 months)

**Free users converting to Pro** (assume 2% conversion):
- Monthly revenue per user: $14.99
- Lifetime (1 year): ~$180
- CAC target: < $18 (breakeven in 1-2 months)

## Cost Structure

### Infrastructure
- GPU instance (AWS g4dn.xlarge): $500/month
- Storage & bandwidth: $200/month
- Stripe fees (2.9% + $0.30): Variable
- Total: ~$700-1,500/month

### Margins
- First 20 paid users break even infrastructure
- 100+ users: 60%+ gross margin

## Growth Hacks

1. **Referral Program**: Give $10 credit for each referral
2. **GitHub Sponsors**: Accept donations, offer perks
3. **Affiliate Program**: 20% commission for referrals
4. **Developer Community**: Sponsor hackathons, competitions
5. **Freemium Upsells**: Limit free tier just enough to push upgrades

## Success Metrics

- **Activation**: % of signups trying API
- **Retention**: % of users making requests month 2+
- **Conversion**: % of free users upgrading
- **Expansion**: % of users upgrading from Starter → Pro
- **CAC**: Cost to acquire one paying customer
- **LTV**: Total revenue per customer
- **Churn**: % of paying users canceling

**Target KPIs Year 1**:
- Activation: 40%
- Retention: 30%
- Conversion: 10%
- Churn: < 5% monthly
- CAC: $5
- LTV: $200+
